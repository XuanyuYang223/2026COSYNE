In a descriptive reanalysis of six frozen RNNs, cross-context wrong-content patches on queries with three pairwise-distinct mathematical candidates matched donor-content/donor-rule answers in 97.393137%, receiver-content/receiver-rule answers in 0.433758%, and donor-content/receiver-rule recombinations in 0.954218% (equal-weight seed means; 3,615,840 expanded eligible queries). Patch outputs agreed with actual donor-native predictions in 99.999668%, reproducing all 10,472 unique donor-native error queries (94,248 expanded records); these descriptive rescoring results on previously opened interventions are not independent confirmation.
