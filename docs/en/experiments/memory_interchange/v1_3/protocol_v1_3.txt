Four. Retain both all-sample and native-correct analyses

Original primary metric unchanged:
L_all=mean[max(0,ell_swap-ell_native)]
R_all=mean[native correct and swapped incorrect].
Also report native/swapped accuracy, signed CE changes and post-swap repair of native errors, separately by direction.

Add prescribed conditional analyses:
E_rec: receiver is correct without intervention.
E_both: E_rec and matched donor correct under its own context for identical visible S/query.
Donor native truth uses donor context; donor and receiver correct answers need not coincide.
Determine subsets from unperturbed outputs and freeze masks before inspecting swap outputs.

Report:
L_rec: positive CE damage on E_rec;
R_rec: swapped-error proportion on E_rec;
L_both/R_both: corresponding metrics on E_both;
Retained mask counts/fractions.

The receiver/matched-donor-defined mask applies to both matched and wrong-content swaps; never select separate favorable treatment samples.
A stricter three-native-correct sensitivity mask may require receiver, matched donor and wrong-content donor all correct on their own natural tasks; both treatments share it.
Wrong-content donor correctness does not establish compatibility; it only checks the donor is not wholly failing its own task.

Always show all-sample analysis, not only successful trials.
Conditional analysis asks whether replacement disrupts behavior where the system could originally answer correctly; it does not establish interchangeability for every task/state.
R_all/R_rec denominators differ and must be explicit. Native errors cannot be counted directly as new interchange errors.

Five. Diagnose initial versus current content

Match task-defined current q without assuming that m stores it in unified coordinates.
The network may rely on initial p and operation history to compute at readout, or use history/context-dependent content codes.

For every a→b matched intervention, known quantities include:
Donor initial permutation p_a;
Receiver prefix net transformation G_b;
Receiver continuation net transformation F_b.

Two prespecified counterfactuals:
Reusable-current-content account: q_future=F_b(q).
Initial-input/history-dependent account: q_initial_cf=F_b(G_b(p_a)).

Record patch agreement with both full mappings and single-query answers separately.
Coincident candidates cannot distinguish these accounts. Report all queries, answer-distinct subsets and full-mapping overlaps separately.
Use both directions; stratify by same/cross context, H, S and d.
Do not display only q values favoring an account.

Greater patch agreement with q_initial_cf is consistent with initial-input/history-dependent encoding, but alone proves neither universally delayed computation nor absence of history-dependent memory coordinates.
Greater agreement with F_b(q) supports content compatibility under receiver control, not biological mechanisms or abstract group structure.

Add two fixed diagnostic content probes:
From the same m, predict initial p_a and current q n×n one-hot representations separately.
Fit only balanced natural T states with ridge alpha=1, identical samples, preprocessing and budgets.
Compare V per-item/full-mapping decoding.
Probe results do not train networks, define interchangeable subspaces, select checkpoints or modify architecture.
Easier linear decoding of initial content establishes readability differences, not the information actually used by the network.

Exploratory V trends may be described, but not single-model large-sample p-values, cross-seed generalization or plot-based selection of the most effective similarity metric.
Retain dependencies among queries sharing states/orbits.

All outcomes are permitted:
Broad matched-swap success: widespread compatibility, potentially a CKA-prediction ceiling effect.
Severe matched-swap failure: possible control/history-dependent encoding, interpreted with initial-input counterfactuals; no automatic model changes.
Matched exceeds wrong donor: content-related intervention relevance, not automatic CKA incremental prediction.
Matched equals wrong donor: interpret native quality, counterfactuals and controls; do not automatically declare an implementation bug.
Confidence changes without accuracy changes: explicitly distinguish probability damage from unchanged behavior.

Formal execution cannot depend on positive V H1 or attractive V scatters. Do not select only networks yielding the expected scientific conclusion.
V interprets/checks the original intervention, not training or metric changes. Any later V-driven primary-task revision requires a new version, disclosed exploration history and refreezing before confirmation.
